# Optional Profile — AI × Finance


## AGENDA-01 — Profile scope, not a personal research agenda
This profile studies how AI, LLMs, agents, Skills, and automation may change the acquisition, processing, production, validation, use, intermediation, and aggregation of financial information, and how those changes enter decisions or markets when direct evidence exists.

Do not encode the maintainer's personal degree, dissertation, or long-run research agenda into the installed profile.

## AGENDA-02 — Signal → adoption → decision → market ladder
Use the ladder:

```text
AI_OUTPUT_OR_CAPABILITY
→ USER_ADOPTION
→ DECISION_RELIANCE
→ HOLDINGS_OR_TRADES
→ MARKET_OUTCOME
```

Moving to a higher level requires direct evidence or a credible design for that level. Similar AI outputs do not by themselves establish crowded trading. A market-level rollout/outage can identify a market-level availability effect without user logs, but the claim cannot then identify who used AI.

## AGENDA-03 — Entry requirement for market-impact claims
A market-impact project needs at least one direct bridge such as:

```text
OBSERVED_AI_ADOPTION
EXOGENOUS_ROLLOUT_OR_OUTAGE
USER_LEVEL_AI_USE
HOLDINGS_OR_TRADES
PLATFORM_USAGE
ORGANIZATIONAL_ADOPTION_RECORD
```

Without such a bridge, keep the project at the level of capability, output, or information processing.

## AGENDA-04 — AI-system identity is dynamic
Do not treat the following as automatically equivalent constructs:

```text
LIVE_CONSUMER_PRODUCT
LIVE_API
CONTROLLED_RAG
OPEN_WEIGHT_MODEL
LOCAL_AGENT
STATIC_REPLAY
```

When substituting one for another, compare actor exposure, system instructions, information set, retrieval universe, tools, personalization, account/region if material, latency, reproducibility, and the external-validity target. A construct-changing substitution requires re-freezing the research design.

## AGENDA-05 — Snapshot live AI systems
For research that depends on a live AI system, record the material elements that determine reproducibility and exposure, such as provider, product, exposed model/version when available, access mode, system instructions, prompt, tools, retrieval mode, sampling parameters, account/region if material, run time, repetition plan, retained raw outputs/citations, and vendor change information.

If the provider does not expose an internal model/version, record `NOT_EXPOSED_BY_PROVIDER`; do not guess.

## AGENDA-06 — Historical leakage and memorization risk
When a current model is used on a historical task, record the model release date, known training cutoff if disclosed, possible pretraining exposure, web/retrieval exposure, point-in-time input controls, future leakage risk, and model-version reproducibility. If memorization cannot be ruled out, lower the claim ceiling.

## AGENDA-07 — Evaluation independence
If the main quality judgment is produced by an LLM from the same provider or model family as the system under study, require at least one independent anchor when feasible:

```text
OBJECTIVE_FINANCE_TARGET
HUMAN_EXPERT_LABEL
REPRODUCIBLE_RULE_BASED_METRIC
EXTERNAL_REALIZED_OUTCOME
```

## AGENDA-08 — AI capability is not synonymous with NLP
Route the method to the actual bottleneck: text, entity resolution, graph structure, multimodality, causal heterogeneity, simulation, scalable coding, high-frequency processing, or another capability. The novelty of an AI tool is not itself a finance contribution.

## AGENDA-09 — A benchmark result is not automatically a finance paper
A leaderboard, model ranking, or generic claim that one agent/model architecture performs better is normally an engineering result. Retain it as a finance contribution only when it changes an economic inference, mechanism, boundary, institution, or decision.

## AGENDA-10 — Blind-first agenda discovery when applicable
When blind-first idea discovery is used: begin with the current problem domain and general access boundary; avoid loading old candidate substance before the blind problem/candidate pool is frozen; then compare historical projects for collision; evaluate researcher-originated ideas under the same standard.

If historical context cannot be avoided, record `BLIND_CONTEXT_CONTAMINATION=POSSIBLE`. Do not reward or penalize a candidate merely because it resembles an old seed.

## AGENDA-11 — Agenda-stage outputs and transition
An agenda/landscape stage should produce problem maps, literature ecology, bibliography/search log, candidate families, collision/source maps, lifecycle coverage, and a shortlist or stage matrix. It does not by itself authorize a jump to deep collision adjudication or empirical execution.

## AGENDA-12 — Candidate-family rule
For candidates sharing a mother problem, compare actor, friction, AI change, observable object, outcome, closest literature, data, and claim ceiling. Classify them as `MERGE_AS_ONE_FAMILY`, `PRIMARY_WITH_NESTED_VARIANT`, or `DISTINCT_CANDIDATES` before duplicating downstream work.

## AGENDA-13 — Finance-specific increment
If a proposed AI mechanism would make essentially the same contribution after replacing finance with law, medicine, or generic software use, identify the finance-specific institution, contract, payoff, price-formation process, capital-allocation problem, disclosure/regulatory structure, or other boundary that changes the inference. Otherwise treat it as a general AI application rather than a finance contribution.

## AGENDA-14 — Project-specific AI claims live in project artifacts
Provider names, outage events, model lists, event rules, and project-specific implementation details belong in the project freeze/registry, not in this reusable profile.

## AGENDA-15 — Finance-program and field-home gates
Use the three field-home rules below before deep literature adjudication for an AI × Finance candidate.

## AI-FIN-FIELD01 — Finance legibility
A candidate that enters the finance-priority queue should be able to answer in one sentence: `What finance question changes if this result is correct?`

The answer should connect to a finance object such as prices/returns, financing/investment, governance, information intermediation, banking/credit, risk/insurance, household finance, market design, or liquidity.

## AI-FIN-FIELD02 — Academic home
Before deep collision adjudication, identify a `PRIMARY_ACADEMIC_HOME` and a small set of canonical or nearest papers from that home. HCI/CS mechanisms may be adjacent evidence but do not substitute for the finance-specific increment.

## AI-FIN-FIELD03 — Domain familiarity
Lack of prior familiarity is not an automatic stop. If the project requires substantial institutional knowledge, create a minimal `DOMAIN_PRIMER`. If the learning burden exceeds the current feasible budget, lower executability rather than pretending the research opportunity is weaker.

## AGENDA-16 — General versus agenda-challenge coverage
Cross-domain challenge searches are activated when the project is explicitly in an agenda-challenge mode or when the current agenda lock is reopened. Within an active AI × Finance agenda, a modality-exclusion challenger may be used if search is being captured by one data modality, but that alone does not justify abandoning the agenda.

## AGENDA-17 — Mechanism analogue
For general AI mechanisms such as productivity, homogenization, common error, cognitive outsourcing, or coordination, search relevant adjacent fields when useful. The existence of a cross-domain analogue does not automatically eliminate the finance project; the candidate must explain which finance institution, equilibrium, or boundary changes the inference.

## AGENDA-18 — Individual, collective, and rationality claims
Require joint examination of individual quality and collective diversity/error correlation only when the project claims effects of shared infrastructure, standardization, or diversity. Distinguish `HUMAN_RATIONALITY`, `HUMAN_AI_SYSTEM_PERFORMANCE`, and `MARKET_EFFICIENCY`; do not move across levels without evidence.

## AGENDA-19 — Authentic finance outcomes
Investment intention, confidence, and hypothetical portfolio weights are not automatically equivalent to realized financial outcomes. Prefer incentive-compatible decisions, objective forecast/valuation targets, realized outcomes, or professional tasks with external keys when the claim requires real economic behavior. Otherwise reduce the claim ceiling.

## AGENDA-20 — Blind-map-driven frontier search
In blind-first discovery, do not seed frontier searches with mechanism terms from old projects before the blind problem map is frozen. Derive frontier queries from the frozen problem families.

## AGENDA-21 — Agenda challenge trigger
Reopen a broad finance/business search only when a documented trigger applies, such as repeated full agenda censuses without a credible survivor, repeated structural infeasibility across independent candidate families, or an explicit user request.

## AGENDA-22 — External discovery handoff
A candidate discovered outside ScholarOps may enter focused pre-admission screening with its upstream provenance marked preliminary. Do not rerun a full landscape merely to fit an old schema, and do not promote the handoff directly into deep adjudication or empirical work without the missing gates.

## AGENDA-23 — Neutral lifecycle coverage
When the agenda is AI and the financial-information lifecycle, cover acquisition, processing, production, validation, use, intermediation, and aggregation neutrally. A lifecycle layer may be classified as saturated, no actionable gap, or hold for future data; do not invent a candidate to fill every box.

## AGENDA-24 — Direction-neutral candidate judgment
Do not reward candidates merely because they fit an attractive story such as "AI improves efficiency" or "AI increases homogenization." Judge candidates using problem importance, evidence, construct validity, identification, executability, finance significance, and the value of a decisive negative result. Positive, negative, and null outcomes should be interpretable ex ante.

## AGENDA-25 — Architecture decomposition
Model, data, tool, Skill, planning, verification, and multi-agent choices are production inputs, not contribution categories by themselves. If changing the architecture changes only engineering performance, downgrade the finance contribution; retain it only when it changes a financial inference, mechanism, or allocation decision.
